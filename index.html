    KNOWLEDGE BASE & FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about settlements, activation, sandbox testing, and global compliance.

    01

    How fast are funds deposited into my bank account?

    Standard settlements occur on a T+1 or T+2 business day schedule. Growth and Enterprise merchants can enable Instant Settlements (T+0) to receive payouts in as little as 15 minutes, 24/7/365 including bank holidays.

    02

    What documents are required for business activation?

    Company PAN, Certificate of Incorporation / GST Registration, Authorized Signatory ID (PAN or Aadhaar), and a cancelled cheque or bank statement for automated penny-drop verification. Digital onboarding takes less than 10 minutes.

    03

    Can I test everything before submitting KYC?

    Yes! Test mode is completely unrestricted. You can simulate UPI payments, test 3DS card outcomes, and verify webhook signatures against our mock banking sandbox without submitting any documentation.

    04

    How does Toucan handle international payments & 130+ currencies?

    Toucan provides native multi-currency pricing (MCP) and localized checkout in 130+ fiat currencies with real-time conversion rates. Settlements are automatically converted and disbursed into your domestic INR nodal bank account with automated FIRC certificates.

    05

    What fraud prevention and 3DS 2.0 protocols are included?

    Every transaction is screened by our real-time ML risk engine analyzing 120+ behavioral parameters, device fingerprints, and velocity patterns. EMV 3DS 2.0 provides frictionless zero-step authentication on low-risk payments while maintaining bank-grade PCI-DSS Level 1 protection.
