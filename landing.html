Toucan

A comprehensive payments suite in India designed to help modern businesses seamlessly accept, process, and disburse payments. Toucan gives you unified access to all payment modes including credit cards, debit cards, netbanking across 58+ banks, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED), and popular digital wallets including JioMoney, Mobikwik, Airtel Money, FreeCharge, and Ola Money.

ToucanX supercharges your business banking experience, bringing effectiveness, efficiency, and real-time visibility to all financial processes. With ToucanX, businesses get access to fully-functional current accounts, supercharged instant payouts, automated reconciliation, and hands-free payroll compliance.

Manage your marketplace splits, automate vendor bank transfers, collect recurring subscriptions, share branded invoices with customers, and avail working capital facilities — all from a single platform. Fast forward your business with Toucan.

Disclaimer: The ToucanX powered financial products are provided in direct partnership with RBI-licensed partner banks, in accordance with RBI regulations.

  • Corporate Credit Card: Issued by YES Bank Ltd. and RBL Bank Ltd. on the Mastercard network.
  • Current Accounts & Escrow: Provided by Axis Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank, and RBL Bank Ltd.
